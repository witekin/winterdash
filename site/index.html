Open-source ESP32 firmware

Know what your Victron Blue Smart charger is doing — at a glance, all winter.

A tiny gadget that reads your charger over Bluetooth and shows it on a little screen, a web page on your phone, and in Home Assistant — for a car, motorcycle, or boat battery.

Install in your browser Read the docs

Chrome or Edge on a desktop · no app, nothing to install on your computer.

Features

What it does.

Live charger readings

Voltage, current, and charge stage (Bulk / Absorption / Float) read straight from the charger's Bluetooth broadcast. Nothing wired to the charger.

Your own banner

Put your actual car, motorcycle, or boat on the screen — the built-in converter turns any photo into a banner, right on the device.

Web & Home Assistant

Open a web page from your phone on your own network — and it shows up in Home Assistant automatically, no key to enter.

Cheap hardware

Runs on a €7–20 ESP32 board you can buy anywhere. No proprietary hardware, no subscription.

Free & open-source

GPL-3.0. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — everything runs on your own device, on your own network.

Demo mode

A realistic charge cycle plays with no charger connected, so you can try the whole thing before you pair anything.
